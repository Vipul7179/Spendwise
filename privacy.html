Terms and Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Hisbo is a personal expense tracker made by Vipul Singh. This page explains in plain language what we collect, how we use it, and the rules for using the app.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is stored in scrambled form, so we cannot read it.
  • What you add to the app: expenses, categories, budgets, savings goals, recurring items, and group names, member names and shared bills. If you choose to save phone numbers for reminders, those too.
  • Basic technical data: your last activity time and standard logs kept by our hosting providers.

How we use it

  • To run the app and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To send you account emails, such as a welcome email and password reset.
  • To count overall usage, such as the number of users, so we can improve the app.

We never sell your data

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Hisbo, and when the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a database provided by Supabase. The app is hosted on Vercel and emails are sent through Resend. Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules ensure each signed-in user can read only their own data.

Guest mode

If you use Hisbo without an account, your data stays only in your own browser on your device and is not sent to us.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your entries at any time inside the app.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all stored data by contacting the developer using the links below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service can promise perfect security. Please choose a strong password and do not share it.

Children

Hisbo is not meant for children under 18.
